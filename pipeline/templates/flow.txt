Hard rules for why_the_flow_works. A leak check rejects the flow otherwise:
  - Describe the design, never the content. No client, competition, team, school, year, person, company,
    brand, product or place names, and no hint that identifies them.
  - No digits at all (write "one big number", not "3x").
  - Never copy three or more consecutive words from the slide's text.
  - No brand identity. No specific colour names (red, blue, coral, teal, navy, gold ... or "-ish" forms): use
    roles (primary, accent, brand-colour fill, dark, light, mid-grey, black, white, greyscale, tint, shade,
    saturated, muted).

    portrait, domain or URL, and no "brand art", "brand illustration", "brand motif" or "brand swoosh". Write
    "headshot slots" for portraits; "logo" is fine as a placement ("logo top right").
  - Never say what an illustration depicts (botanical, an animal, a character, a landmark, a skyline, a world
    map): write "illustration", "photo", "icon", "image" or "map".
