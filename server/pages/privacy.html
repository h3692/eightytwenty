Privacy

eightytwenty is a read-only connector with no accounts. This page says what it receives and what it keeps.

What a request sends

The tool arguments your assistant passes: the search text and filters for find_references and find_deck_flows, a stage name for get_playbook, or an id for get_reference. Nothing else from your chat or your files is sent.

What is kept

  • Your IP address is used only for rate limits. It sits in a request counter that is deleted 24 hours after your last request. Requests that come from claude.ai arrive from Anthropic's servers and share one counter, so your own address is not seen.
  • Search text is not logged. To return identical results for identical searches, results are cached under a one-way hash of the normalised search text and filters, never the text itself. Cached results are dropped when the library is rebuilt.
  • No request logs. The server's request logging is switched off.

What is not used

No accounts, no cookies, no analytics or tracking, and no third-party scripts, fonts or images on these pages.

Infrastructure

The connector runs on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare processes each request to deliver it and may keep its own operational data under its privacy policy.
